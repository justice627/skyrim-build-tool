# Skyrim roleplay/challenge concepts, perk templates and signature gear (for the build-reference tool)

Scope note: Lore characters and named creator build series are covered by other researchers and are excluded here. Mapping judgements ("Maps to") refer to the 56 existing builds plus 10 pure-race builds in `_existing_builds.md`. Several fetches failed (UESP Skyrim:Perks, Fandom Creation Club list), and fetched pages gave limited detail, so many requested concepts could not be cited. They are listed under Gaps and were not invented.

## Which roleplay/challenge concepts are most widely discussed?

### Takeaway
The most-cited roleplay and challenge concepts in outlet roundups are restriction runs: fists only, pacifist, Illusion only, Flames only, conjured or crafted or stolen or purchased gear only, tools only, and level 1. There are also "weird" builds such as poisoner, staff and scroll wizard, shield-caster "Spellshield", Alteration utility mage, Illusion/Conjuration support, rune-and-spider-scroll trapper, the legendary wooden sword and the "melee chef". Most of them need only vanilla or DLC. The unarmed, pacifist, Illusion-only, poisoner and Spellshield concepts would each be a real new addition to the tool. Most of the others work better as challenge "modifiers" attached to existing builds.

### Cited Findings
**Challenge runs (Game Rant, "Most insane challenge runs ever ranked").** The article's title promises 15, but only 10 are in the body.
- **Fists Only (Senza):** fight unarmed, with no damage-boosting special gauntlets. Fights are long, so running from most enemies is practical. The article calls the Khajiit unarmed racial bonus too weak to build around — [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/skyrim-most-insane-challenge-runs-ever-ranked/)
- **Illusion Magic Only (G00SE_IT):** a High Elf starts with Fury, which turns enemies on each other or lures them into traps — [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/skyrim-most-insane-challenge-runs-ever-ranked/)
- **Conjured Equipment Only (Mah Dry Bread):** only bound or summoned gear. The early game is hard and Conjuration perks ease it — [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/skyrim-most-insane-challenge-runs-ever-ranked/)
- **Flames Spell Only (Nerbit):** only the novice Destruction spell Flames — [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/skyrim-most-insane-challenge-runs-ever-ranked/)
- **Stolen Items Only (DangerBen):** recommended at Legendary difficulty as a "true thief build" — [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/skyrim-most-insane-challenge-runs-ever-ranked/)
- **Purchased Items Only** and **Crafted Items Only (BADCompanySarge):** crafted items scale with the player — [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/skyrim-most-insane-challenge-runs-ever-ranked/)
- **Pure Combat Run (MajorSlackAttack):** no crafting or buying, loot only, at Legendary. Goals are to reach the armor cap and level 80 One-Handed — [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/skyrim-most-insane-challenge-runs-ever-ranked/)
- **Tools Only (G00SE_IT):** Woodcutter's Axe and Pickaxe as weapons — [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/skyrim-most-insane-challenge-runs-ever-ranked/)
- **Level 1 Run (The Spiffing Brit):** never raise any skill — [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/skyrim-most-insane-challenge-runs-ever-ranked/)

**Unique or weird builds (TheGamer, "Skyrim weirdest unique builds").**
- **Unarmed build:** Heavy Armor and Enchanting. Uses the Fists of Steel perk (Heavy Armor, which adds gauntlet armor rating to unarmed damage), Daedric gauntlets, and a ring and gloves enchanted with Fortify Unarmed. Khajiit is suggested. It is viable on everything except Legendary — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/skyrim-weirdest-unique-builds/)
- **Pacifist Rogue:** Sneak, Light Armor, Pickpocket and Illusion, with no weapons. Uses Muffle, Pacify, Throw Voice and the Whirlwind Sprint shout. A companion has to kill essential targets in some quests, and some guild questlines cannot be finished — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/skyrim-weirdest-unique-builds/)
- **Poisoner:** primary skills Alchemy, Sneak and Pickpocket; secondary Restoration, Illusion and Light Armor. Uses daggers, crafted poisons, and the Poisoned perk (Pickpocket) to plant poisons on targets. Also Poison Rune, Paralysis, the Serpent Stone and Mora's Agony (Black Book). It is weak early and strong late — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/skyrim-weirdest-unique-builds/)
- **Spellshield:** Destruction, Block and Heavy Armor, with Restoration, Illusion and Conjuration secondary. Uses the Spellbreaker shield, Frost Cloak, Chain Lightning, Frenzy and Greater Ward. Shield bashes open enemies up for spells, and it uses no melee weapon. The article rates it "excellent on all difficulties" — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/skyrim-weirdest-unique-builds/)
- **Dumb Wizard:** fights only with staves and scrolls (one staff per school, plus Spider Scrolls). A Conjuration staff with Soul Trap recharges the others. Usable up to Master difficulty — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/skyrim-weirdest-unique-builds/)
- **Alteration Mage (utility/pacifist):** Alteration and Light Armor, with Illusion secondary. Uses Paralysis, Telekinesis, the Detect spells and the Disarm shout. It is weak in hard fights — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/skyrim-weirdest-unique-builds/)
- **Illusion Support ("puppet master"-adjacent):** Illusion and Conjuration, with Speech and armor secondary. Buffs allies and thralls with Dead Thrall, Frost Atronach, Call to Arms and the Ritual Stone — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/skyrim-weirdest-unique-builds/)
- **Spider Mastermind:** Destruction and Alteration. Uses Spider Scrolls (Dragonborn DLC, White Ridge Barrow) and Ash, Fire, Frost and Lightning Runes as a dungeon trapper. Solid on all difficulties except Legendary — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/skyrim-weirdest-unique-builds/)
- **Legendary Wooden Sword:** Smithing, Enchanting and One-Handed. A Wooden Sword is tempered to Legendary and enchanted (for example with chaos damage and health absorb) — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/skyrim-weirdest-unique-builds/)
- **Melee Chef:** Alchemy, Enchanting and One-Handed. Uses fork and knife weapons, paralysis poisons, Vegetable Soup and the Ring of Namira, with vampirism optional — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/skyrim-weirdest-unique-builds/)

## What do community guides consider the essential perks per skill tree, and what are the perk budgets?

### Takeaway
Perk points are one per level, so roughly 49 at level 50 and 80 at level 81. Unlocking all 251 perks requires level 252, which is possible only through Legendary skill resets. Waking Dreams (Dragonborn DLC) refunds a tree's perks for a dragon soul. Outlets repeatedly name a small set of "must-have" perks. Per-tree templates were only partly sourced.

### Cited Findings
**Perk budgets.**
- You get 1 perk point per level. Unlocking every perk needs 251 points, reached at level 252 — [Fextralife Perks](https://skyrim.wiki.fextralife.com/Perks)
- Making a skill Legendary at 100 resets it to 15 and refunds its perks — [Fextralife Perks](https://skyrim.wiki.fextralife.com/Perks)
- The Black Book Waking Dreams (Temple of Miraak) resets one tree and refunds its perk points for a dragon soul — [eXputer](https://exputer.com/guides/black-book-in-skyrim/); [Fextralife Perks](https://skyrim.wiki.fextralife.com/Perks)
- Magic-tree perk counts: Alteration 14, Conjuration 16, Destruction 17, Illusion 13, Restoration 13 and Enchanting 13, for 86 in total — [Fextralife Perks](https://skyrim.wiki.fextralife.com/Perks)

**Recommended perks (GamesRadar).**
- **Master Trader** (Speech 100): every merchant gets 1,000 more gold — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/skyrim-perks-best-guide)
- **Extra Pockets** (Pickpocket 50): +100 carry weight — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/skyrim-perks-best-guide)
- **Unbreakable** (Lockpicking 100): lockpicks never break — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/skyrim-perks-best-guide)
- **Assassin's Blade** (Sneak): the article says dual daggers can deal "fifteen times normal damage while sneaking" — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/skyrim-perks-best-guide)
- **Silent Roll** (Sneak) — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/skyrim-perks-best-guide)
- **Arcane Blacksmith** (Smithing), paired with **Extra Effect** (Enchanting 100) — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/skyrim-perks-best-guide)
- **Quiet Casting** (Illusion) and **Soul Siphon** (Enchanting) are also mentioned — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/skyrim-perks-best-guide)

**Archetype key perks found in build articles.**
- **Fists of Steel** (Heavy Armor) for unarmed — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/skyrim-weirdest-unique-builds/)
- **Poisoned** (Pickpocket) for poisoners — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/skyrim-weirdest-unique-builds/)
- **Necromancer** and **Dark Souls** (Conjuration) are required for the Necromantic Grimoire "Empowered Necromancy" enchantment — [Fandom: Necromantic Grimoire](https://elderscrolls.fandom.com/wiki/Necromantic_Grimoire)

### Inferences
- At level 50 (about 49 points) a build can realistically fill two or three trees deeply. At level 81 (about 80 points) it can cover four or five. This is my arithmetic from the 1-point-per-level rule above.
- The tool's planned NPC-derived perks could be supplemented by a "key perks" chip per archetype using the cited perks above.

## Which signature gear sets, AE/Creation Club items, Black Book powers and shouts anchor builds, and which need only vanilla/DLC?

### Takeaway
Within the AE/Creation Club content verified this pass, Necromantic Grimoire and the Arcane Archer Pack are the clearest examples of content that enables a new playstyle. Necromantic Grimoire turns the Conjurer's Necromancer variant into a full summoner kit; the Arcane Archer Pack adds a bound quiver and elemental and soul arrows. Spell Knight Armor is a heavy spellsword kit. Ghosts of the Tribunal adds Ordinator armor and Tribunal weapons. Black Books (Dragonborn DLC) provide strong build anchors without any Creation Club content.

### Cited Findings
**AE / Creation Club scope.**
- All 74 Creations are included in the Anniversary Edition. Fishing, Rare Curios, Survival Mode and Saints & Seducers are free to Special Edition (and Switch) owners — [UESP Creations](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Creations)
- New Creations added with AE include: Fearsome Fists, Ghosts of the Tribunal, Necromantic Grimoire, Bow of Shadows, Goldbrand, Headman's Cleaver, Redguard Elite Armaments, Staves, The Cause, Bloodchill Manor, Gallows Hall, Nchuanthumz, and the Alternative Armors series — [UESP Anniversary Edition](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Anniversary_Edition)

**Necromantic Grimoire (AE/CC).**
- **Spells:** Conjure Skeleton Minion, Marksman, Warlock and Champion; Conjure Ancient Deathpriest; Conjure Bone Colossus; Conjure Cursed Spectre, Haunting Spirit, Sundered Shade, Tomb Guardian and Undying Ghost; Command Undead; Banish Undead; Necromancer's Ritual; Soul Split — [Fandom](https://elderscrolls.fandom.com/wiki/Necromantic_Grimoire)
- **Armor:** necromancer robe and hood sets, including Ascendant, Elite and Runic robes — [Fandom](https://elderscrolls.fandom.com/wiki/Necromantic_Grimoire)
- **Enchantments:** "Dark Moon" adds Magicka but reduces healing rate by 30%. "Empowered Necromancy" lets you have one more lesser summon or reanimation and needs the Necromancer and Dark Souls perks — [Fandom](https://elderscrolls.fandom.com/wiki/Necromantic_Grimoire)
- **Maps to:** the Conjurer's "Necromancer" variant. Suggest attaching it as a gear and spell kit, or promoting it to a full build.

**Arcane Archer Pack (CC).**
- Adds Fire, Ice, Lightning, Bone, Telekinesis and Soul Stealer arrows, a Bound Quiver power, and the Telekinesis Arrows lesser power. Bone arrows need the Dragon Armor perk — [UESP](https://content1.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Arcane_Archer_Pack)
- **Maps to:** Battle archer (mage-archer). Suggest attaching it as a gear kit.

**Spell Knight Armor (CC).**
- A heavy armor set in Iron, Steel and Ebony versions. One Ebony set gives: +25 Heavy Armor (cuirass), +70 stamina (boots), −25% Restoration cost (gauntlets) and 22% magic resistance (helmet). The Ebony set from Simon Rodayne's grave gives: +40% one-handed damage, +50 carry weight, −25% Destruction cost, and +70 Magicka. Acquired through the "Crypt of the Heart" quest — [UESP Spell Knight Armor Items](https://content1.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Spell_Knight_Armor_Items)
- **Maps to:** Spellsword (heavy armor, one-handed, Destruction, Restoration). This is a near-perfect gear kit for it.

**Ghosts of the Tribunal (AE/CC, Solstheim).**
- Adds Ordinator armor, the Masks of the Tribunal, and the weapons Hopesfire, Trueflame, Magebane, Cleaver of St. Felms and Skull Crusher. The quest starts from the Heretic Dossier at the Raven Rock temple — [UESP](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Ghosts_of_the_Tribunal)
- **Maps to:** a possible new "Ordinator / Tribunal zealot" concept, or a gear kit for Vigilant of Stendarr or Temple priest.

**Black Book powers (Dragonborn DLC, no Creation Club needed).** All entries below are from [eXputer](https://exputer.com/guides/black-book-in-skyrim/).
- **Epistolary Acumen:** Dragonborn Force, Flame or Frost. These are shout upgrades and are the anchor for a "shouts-focused Dragonborn".
- **Filament and Filigree:** Secret of Arcana (spells free for 30 s), Secret of Protection (half damage for 30 s), Secret of Strength (power attacks cost no stamina for 30 s).
- **The Hidden Twilight:** Mora's Agony, Mora's Boon, Mora's Grasp.
- **The Sallow Regent:** Seeker of Might, Shadows or Sorcery. Each gives 10% to its archetype and 10% to a crafting skill.
- **The Winds of Change:** Companion's Insight, Lover's Insight, Scholar's Insight.
- **Untold Legends:** Bardic Knowledge, Black Market, Secret Servant.
- **Waking Dreams:** perk respec.

### Inferences
- **Builds that need AE/Creation Club:** a full necromancer (Necromantic Grimoire), an elemental arcane archer (Arcane Archer Pack), an Ordinator / Tribunal zealot (Ghosts of the Tribunal), and a heavy Spell Knight kit (Spell Knight Armor).
- **Vanilla/DLC only:** unarmed, pacifist, poisoner, Spellshield, rune trapper (Dragonborn DLC), the Black Book anchors (Dragonborn DLC), and the vampire hunter with Light of Day. Light of Day is CC per the Game Rant AE list; the vanilla Dawnguard Crossbow route was not verified this pass.
- Gear kits fit existing builds better as attachments than as new builds:
  - Spell Knight Armor → Spellsword
  - Arcane Archer Pack → Battle archer
  - Necromantic Grimoire → Conjurer/Necromancer
  - Stendarr's Hammer → Vigilant of Stendarr

- The Spell Knight Armor's spell-based scaling mechanic (if any) is not described on UESP's items page. Its "Ebony first variant" acquisition is unstated.
- Daedric artifact sets per Prince, Aedric shrine blessings, unique armor sets (Nightingale, Ancient Shrouded, Deathbrand, Stalhrim, Dragonpriest masks) and Telvanni/Psijic/Dwemer gear were not researched due to the fetch budget.
