# Community-published named Skyrim builds (ledger for the build-reference tool)

Scope note: ~21 tool calls. YouTube video pages (Fudgemuppet's own descriptions), Reddit, Nexus and Fandom all blocked or refused the fetches, so most build details come from two secondary sources that summarise creators' videos and credit them by embedded video: GamersDecide (three list articles) and TheGamer. Fudgemuppet's catalogue of names comes from a fan-made "spin the wheel" page, so treat it as a name list only. I did not verify any gear item against UESP (see Gaps). "Maps to" judgements are mine and compare against `_existing_builds.md`.

## Which community build series are best known, and what are their most popular named builds?

### Takeaway
FudgeMuppet is the dominant named-build creator. He has made over 100 "The X" builds, and secondary sites credit Skypothesis as the second-best-known creator of lore-heavy named builds. Most widely recurring archetypes (Paladin, Necromancer, Spellsword, Stealth Archer, Battlemage) come from generic listicles, not from a creator.

### Inferences
- Fudgemuppet's builds fit the user's tool best: each has a name, a backstory, race, stone and gear. His naming style ("The X") also fits a ledger.
- Fudgemuppet very often uses Lover early and Atronach or Lord late (Dealer, Madman, Bard, Praying Mantis, Mechanist, Master), and he often uses the Black Star.

### Gaps
- Fudgemuppet's own descriptions, perk lists and stones for most of the 116 names (Pirate, Druid, Witchhunter, Paladin, Necromancer, Ronin, Blademaster, Sorcerer, Undead Corsair, Silver Hand, etc.) were not retrievable. YouTube blocked fetches, so the names are confirmed only via a fan list.
- Brodual, ESO (the YouTube channel) and r/skyrimbuilds top posts could not be surveyed directly. Searches returned only Elder Scrolls Online content, and Reddit was not fetched.
- No UESP/Fandom gear verification was done. Item placements (e.g., Windshear on the Katariah, Gloves of the Pugilist from Gian the Fist) are as the secondary sources state them.

## Which named builds recur across sources?

### Takeaway
Paladin, Necromancer, Spellsword/Battlemage, Stealth Archer, Dual-wield/dagger Assassin, Bard, Barbarian/werewolf, Unarmed Khajiit, Witcher/monster hunter and Druid recur across two or more sources. Sun-mage, pirate and witch-hunter appear only as Fudgemuppet titles, without details.

### Cited Findings
- Paladin appears in [TheGamer list](https://www.thegamer.com/skyrim-most-powerful-builds-fun/), [TheGamer guide](https://www.thegamer.com/elder-scrolls-skyrim-paladin-build-guide/), [GamersDecide 15](https://gamersdecide.com/pc-game-news/10-best-skyrim-builds-2017-3), [GamersDecide 10](https://gamersdecide.com/pc-game-news/10-best-skyrim-builds-2017-1) and the [Fudgemuppet list](https://spinthewheel.app/fudgemuppet-skyrim-builds-116). Templar and Divine Crusader are close kin.
- Necromancer appears in TheGamer (named summons: Wrathman, Storm Atronach, Seeker, with Ordinator/Vokrii recommended), GamersDecide 15 (Ritual Stone, Necromancer Robes/Amulet, Vokun mask, Soul Tear), GamersDecide 10 (BIPOHBOG, Dunmer) and Fudgemuppet ("The Necromancer", "The Bonelord", "The Thrall Master") — sources as above
- Unarmed Khajiit appears as TheGamer's "Pugilist Khajiit" (Fists of Steel, Daedric gauntlets; Marked for Death, Unrelenting Force, Whirlwind Sprint), as GamersDecide 15's "Unarmed Khajiit" and as Fudgemuppet's "Sabre Cat" (same video) — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/skyrim-most-powerful-builds-fun/), [GamersDecide 15](https://gamersdecide.com/pc-game-news/10-best-skyrim-builds-2017-3)
- Stealth Archer: Thief Stone, Bosmer or Khajiit, Muffle, Bound Bow and Aura Whisper — [GamersDecide 15](https://gamersdecide.com/pc-game-news/10-best-skyrim-builds-2017-3), [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/skyrim-most-powerful-builds-fun/)

### Inferences
- Stealth Archer and Dual-Dagger Assassin already map cleanly to the existing Scout and Assassin builds. "Stealth Archer" is the community's name for Scout and could be added as an alias.

### Gaps
- No source with details was found for Sun-mage/Dawnguard vampire-hunter-with-Stendarr's-Aura, Pirate (Fudgemuppet only) or Witch Hunter (Fudgemuppet "The Witchhunter" only).

### Takeaway
Detailed perk lists are rare in secondary sources. Only TheGamer's Paladin guide names perks tree by tree. Stones cluster around Lover leading to Atronach or Lord (Fudgemuppet), plus Atronach (Skypothesis mages), Ritual (necromancers), Thief (archers) and Lord (clerics/tanks).

### Inferences
- The tool could take Fudgemuppet's "early stone → final stone" pattern as a two-stage field.

### Gaps
- No perk-by-perk data for most named builds. That would need the videos or transcripts.

## Vanilla vs DLC/AE/mod dependence

### Takeaway
Most of the ledger works in vanilla or with official DLC: Dawnguard for crossbows, Aetherial items, Soul Tear and the werewolf perk tree; Dragonborn for the Black Star, Stalhrim, Mzund, Cyclone, Dragon Aspect and Black Books. Fudgemuppet's "Remastered" and "Modded" builds, the Bone Shepherd's apparent perk overhaul, and the Ordinator-based Dragonborn depend on mods.

### Cited Findings
- The Sand Demon remaster requires Vokrii, Morningstar, Evenstar, Wintersun and Armors of Velothi — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X-gCJybSQz0)
- "The Celestial – Godlike Modded Build" and "The King – Rogue King Modded Build" are labelled as modded in their titles — [Celestial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2eMScs1g2nE), [King](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c9xmtfixvpY)
- The Dragonborn build relies on Ordinator's Way of the Voice — [GamersDecide 10](https://gamersdecide.com/pc-game-news/10-best-skyrim-builds-2017-1)
- The Vampire Slayer requires Dawnguard — [GamersDecide 15](https://gamersdecide.com/pc-game-news/10-best-skyrim-builds-2017-3)

### Inferences
- No ledger build was found to require Anniversary Edition/Creation Club content. The DLC tags in the ledger are my reading of the listed gear; they are not stated by the sources.

### Gaps
- No AE-specific community builds (e.g., Saints & Seducers, Umbra, Ruined Tower gear) were surfaced in this search.
