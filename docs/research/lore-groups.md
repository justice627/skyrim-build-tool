# Lore groups (ledger G)

Every faction, order and guild UESP files under `Category:Lore-Factions`, checked for how it fights, read through the wiki API on 2026-11-12.

- 734 pages listed; 616 distinct groups after redirects and index pages.
- **134** fighting groups with enough description to build from were read in full (by four research passes, each quote checked against the page). The table below is that ledger.
- **324** fighting groups have pages too thin to say how they fight. They are the information gaps, listed at the end.
- **158** groups do not fight (families, trade guilds, councils) and are left out.

Result: no group needed a new build. Every group that fights matches an existing build's skills. 19 groups became aliases (search finds the build by the group's name) and 44 became lore picks, placed under the build they play like and checked by the validator's skill-fit rule. Lore picks cite their row here as `ref` (G1, G2, ...). Their skill mapping is inferred from the page, so they are graded partly sourced.

Not used: mappings the research itself flagged as weak (a page that names no gear or skills), duplicates of an existing alias or pick (Paladins, Wardens, Dragonknights, Dragonborn), names that fit several builds (Thalmor), and groups that do not fight.
