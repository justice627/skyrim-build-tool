Skyrim Class Builds

The game's playable humanoid NPC classes, read from UESP and turned into player builds you can borrow. Similar classes are grouped into families; near-identical trainer and vendor variants sit inside their parent build. The ten pure race builds close the page.

How to read a build. Each number is the class's skill weight: its share of the skill points an NPC of that class gains every level. A weight of 4 grows twice as fast as a 2. Primary skills have a weight of 3 or more and are the ones to train first and spend most perks on. Secondary skills (weight 2) round the build out. Minor skills (weight 1) are a light touch, often just a few points or a single perk.

Skill tiers: primary is a class weight of 3 or more, secondary 2, minor 1. The level 50 split divides 49 level-ups by the attribute weights. Where an NPC file grows Magicka with no spells (or the reverse), the plan moves those points and says so.
Stones and shouts are suggestions written for this tool unless marked otherwise. "Suggested" means worked out here, not read in a source. "Partly sourced" means a community source names part of the pick. Hover a stone or shout for what it does. DG, HF and DB badges mean the build's picks need Dawnguard, Hearthfire or Dragonborn; no badge means the base game is enough.
Lore and community picks come from the project's research ledger. They describe how other players or the lore shape a build, so they are leads, not game data. Where a picked character's own UESP class sits in another build, the pick says so.
Example NPCs are characters whose UESP page lists that exact class, read through UESP's wiki API. Each is one of several NPCs who share the class, not the only one.
Class weights: UESP, Skyrim:Classes, from your saved PDF and screenshot. Creatures, undead, Daedra, Falmer, giants and werewolf beast forms are left out. Race skill bonuses are the vanilla values; race attribute plans are recommendations.
